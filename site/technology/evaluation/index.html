How the AI review works

A second model checks the first one's work.

01Independent graderA separate model reads each merged change and the rules that applied, and grades each rule as met, missed or not applicable.
02Cause codesEvery miss is labeled with why it happened: rule not loaded, rule unclear, rule ignored, or rule wrong.
03Checked against peopleA sample of grades goes to your reviewers. Their agreement rate is on the scorecard.
04Feeds the next ruleUnclear rules get rewritten, ignored rules get enforcement, wrong rules get retired.

Test harness

Test a rule before it reaches an agent.

01Rule testsEvery failing example must be flagged and every passing example must pass, checked by a linter where one exists and by a model where it does not.
02Paired replayThe same task is attempted with and without the rule in context, and the two results are graded side by side.
03Regression runsApproved rules are rerun against past tasks when a model, a prompt or another rule changes.
04Release gatesGates measure and report. They never activate a rule on their own; a person still decides.
05Scheduled suitesEach suite runs when it is due, from the clock, past runs and the changes since.
