Ask about Business

14-day free trial with 2,000 credits for the organization. A payment card is required to start, and the trial converts to a paid subscription at the end unless you cancel. Prices will be in US dollars and exclude tax. Larger teams and tailored deployments: talk to us.

Credits

Credits pay for model and compute-heavy work.
Everything deterministic is included.

Evaluations, lesson drafting and testing, agent sandbox, shadow and live runs, and model-backed review use credits. Rules, compiled context, the MCP server, host checks and linters never do.

Included monthly

1,000 or 3,000 per seat

Team includes 1,000 credits per seat each month and Business 3,000. Monthly credits do not roll over.

Credit packs

$50, $200 or $1,000

One credit is $0.01, so packs add 5,000, 20,000 or 100,000 credits. Pack credits expire after 12 months. Optional auto top-up has a monthly cap you set.

Priced before it runs

Reserved, refunded, never half run.

CoFlow shows the cost before a run and reserves it. Failed runs are refunded. When the balance is too low, a run is refused rather than half run.

This is the planned launch billing model and is not live. Credit checkout is not active on this website. Your coding-agent subscriptions are separate.
