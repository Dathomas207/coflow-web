Plans, trial and billing

Two plans priced per developer seat, a 14-day trial and monthly credits for model and compute-heavy work.

Status

Planned launch billing model. This page describes the billing model being built for launch. It is not live: CoFlow is in private build, nothing on this website takes payment, and the values below are proposed starting values that may change before launch.

Two plans

CoFlow is priced per developer seat, billed monthly or annually. There are two plans:

PlanSeat priceIncluded creditsWhat it adds
TeamTo be confirmed1,000 per seat per monthStarter rule packs and your own rules, compiled context, the MCP server and CLI, review, the lessons pipeline, Agent Studio and approvals
BusinessTo be confirmed3,000 per seat per monthEverything in Team, plus single sign-on (SSO) and SOC 2 Type II audit support

Seat prices and the discount for annual billing are to be confirmed. Prices will be in US dollars and exclude tax.

14-day free trial

  • The trial lasts 14 days and includes 2,000 credits for the organization.
  • A payment card is required to start the trial.
  • At the end of the trial it converts to a paid subscription on the plan you chose, unless you cancel before then.

Business security

Business includes single sign-on and SOC 2 Type II audit support. Single sign-on needs identity configuration for your organization. Audit support covers readiness and evidence workflows, such as the compliance evidence pack. These docs do not assert that CoFlow holds an issued SOC 2 Type II report.

Read organizations, roles and approvals and RLS and tenant isolation.

Credits in brief

Credits pay for model and compute-heavy work: evaluations, lesson drafting and testing, agent sandbox, shadow and live runs, and model-backed review. Rules, compiled context, the MCP server, host checks and linters never use credits. Monthly credits do not roll over. Credit packs add more. Details are on credits and usage.

During the private build

Pilot terms are agreed with each team directly. The website prepares an enquiry email; it does not sell plans, start trials or take card details. Talk to us about the pilot.
