Scorecard and cost

Whether the rules are loaded, followed and worth what they cost.

The scorecard

The scorecard reports nine metrics in four groups:

GroupMetrics
ControlCoverage, override rate
AdherenceAdherence rate, first-pass adherence, repeat rate
EvaluationReviewer agreement, time to rule
OptimizationContext cost, dead rules

A metric without enough data says “not measured” and why. Reviewer agreement needs 20 findings with a disposition; a rule counts as dead after 90 days without a match. When a rule is missed, the cause points to the fix: not loaded (fix its scope), unclear (rewrite it), ignored (enforce it more strongly) or wrong (retire it). A scheduled job can post the weekly scorecard to chat.

The cost ledger

The ledger records quantities, never prices: input, output and cached tokens, model calls and CI runner minutes, tagged with repository, agent, pack, workflow and pull request. You enter prices from your own contracts. A record without a price is reported as unknown, never as zero.

coflow cost report gives spend per merged pull request, per review, per accepted finding and per approved rule, and the share of spend that produced nothing, such as cancelled runs or rules loaded in every session that never matched.

Budgets

Budgets apply per organization, repository or agent, per day or month. Before spending, an agent asks for a decision:

  • Allow: within budget.
  • Degrade: past the warning line; take the cheaper path.
  • Stop: over budget. The work ends and is reported as partial. A stop never passes a required check.

Savings are tested first

coflow cost advise lists proposed savings, each with its evidence and arithmetic. A saving is adopted only when a harness run on the same pinned dataset shows quality holds: pass rate does not drop, no passing case fails and review time rises by at most 10 percent. Fewer than 20 scored cases is inconclusive. A person decides every proposal. The cost-monitor agent produces the weekly report and proposals.

What is not measured

Cloud infrastructure spend, artifact storage, engineer time and seat licences are not measured. The ledger is separate from CoFlow credits.
