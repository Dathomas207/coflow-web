CoFlow is designed to lower unnecessary token usage and improve adherence to your engineering rules by giving agents the approved instructions that matter for their task.

Token efficiency

Scope instructions by repository, affected files and technology. Use token budgets to keep relevant guidance focused instead of loading every lesson into every task.

A few things to know

Good questions.
Clear answers.

Does CoFlow replace our coding agents?

No. CoFlow gives Claude Code, Codex, Cursor and Copilot a shared, reviewed set of rules.

Where do our rules live?

As plain files in your repository, versioned and reviewed like code. If you stop using CoFlow, you keep the rules.

Who approves what agents learn?

Your team. CoFlow proposes rules from evidence. Your reviewers decide what gets approved.

Do rule packs certify compliance?

No. Mappings to standards help guide engineering work. They do not certify compliance or replace your own review.

Can we try CoFlow today?

The public playground is available without an account. The product is in private build; ask about pilot onboarding.
