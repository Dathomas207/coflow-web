01Who we are and what this covers

This policy covers the getcoflow.ai website, the CoFlow application, and the integrations you connect to it (together, the "Services"). [CoFlow legal entity] operates the Services from [registered address].

For account, billing and website information we act as a controller. For the code, tickets and messages you connect to CoFlow ("Customer Data") we act as a processor for your organization, under our Terms and our Data Processing Addendum (DPA). Where this policy and the DPA differ on Customer Data, the DPA controls.

02Information we collect

You give us

  • Account details: name, work email, organization, role, and the developers you invite.
  • Billing details: billing contact and address. Card payments are handled by our payment processor; we do not store full card numbers.
  • Support messages: what you send us by email or chat.

You connect

  • Code host data: repository contents, pull or merge requests, review comments, check and CI results, and reverts, only for repositories you select.
  • Tracker data: Jira or Linear tickets you choose to connect, used as evidence for rules.
  • Chat data: messages you send to CoFlow, and approvals or rejections, in Slack or Microsoft Teams.
  • Design system data: tokens and component definitions you point us to.

Collected automatically

  • Usage data: sign-ins, feature use, errors and performance metrics in the CoFlow app.
  • Website data: standard server logs such as IP address, browser and pages requested.

03How we use information

  • To run the Services: propose rules from your evidence, compile approved rules for each agent, run plan and pull request checks, and grade adherence.
  • To keep the Services secure, investigate abuse and prevent fraud.
  • To provide support and send service messages such as security notices and billing receipts.
  • To improve the Services using aggregated, deidentified metrics that contain no Customer Data content.
  • To meet legal obligations and enforce our Terms.

We do not sell personal information, and we do not use Customer Data for advertising.

04AI providers and model training

CoFlow uses third-party AI models to read evidence, draft rules and grade changes. We do not use Customer Data to train shared, foundation or third-party models, and we do not let our AI providers do so.

We use AI providers under zero data retention or equivalent no-training terms where the provider offers them. Confirm each listed AI provider is on zero-retention, no-training terms before launch.

05Access to your code

  • CoFlow asks for the narrowest permissions your code host allows for the features you turn on.
  • We read only the repositories you select. You can remove a repository or revoke access at any time from your code host or from CoFlow.
  • Approved rules are stored as files in your own repository. If you stop using CoFlow, you keep them.
  • CoFlow proposes changes as pull or merge requests for your team to approve. It does not merge to your default branch on its own.

06How we share information

  • Subprocessors that help us run the Services, listed below, under written terms that protect the data.
  • The tools you connect, when you tell CoFlow to post a pull request, a chat message or a ticket update.
  • Legal and safety: when required by law, or to protect the rights, property or safety of our users or the public. Where permitted, we tell the affected customer first.
  • Business transfers: as part of a merger, acquisition or asset sale, subject to this policy.

08Retention and deletion

  • Account data: kept while your account is open, then deleted or anonymized unless we must keep it for legal, tax or accounting reasons.
  • Customer Data: after your subscription ends you have 30 days to export it. We then delete it, including from backups, no later than 60 days after the subscription ends.
  • Logs: kept for a limited period for security and debugging, then deleted.

You can ask us to delete Customer Data sooner by writing to privacy@getcoflow.ai.

09Security

  • Data is encrypted in transit with TLS 1.2 or higher and at rest with AES-256.
  • Access to Customer Data is limited to staff who need it to run or support the Services, and is logged.
  • We notify affected customers without undue delay, and within 72 hours of becoming aware, of a security incident affecting their Customer Data.

Security documentation is available to customers on request.

10International transfers

CoFlow is hosted in the United States. When we transfer personal data from the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, we rely on the Standard Contractual Clauses (Module 2) with the UK Addendum and Swiss adjustments, as set out in the DPA.

11Your choices and rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete or export your personal information, to object to or restrict certain processing, and to withdraw consent. Residents of California and other US states with privacy laws have the right to know, delete and correct, and to not be discriminated against for using these rights.

To make a request, email privacy@getcoflow.ai. We will verify your request before acting on it, and you may use an authorized agent. If your information is part of Customer Data, we will pass your request to the organization that controls it and help them respond. You may also complain to your local data protection authority.

12Cookies

The getcoflow.ai website does not use advertising or cross-site tracking cookies. The CoFlow application uses cookies that are needed to sign you in and keep your session secure.

If analytics are added to the site or app, update this section and add a consent banner where required.

13Children

The Services are built for businesses and are not directed to children under 16. We do not knowingly collect their personal information.

14Changes to this policy

We will post any changes here and update the date at the top. If a change is material, we will notify account owners by email at least 30 days before it takes effect.
